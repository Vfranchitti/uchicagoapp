# Ideas for the Part 2 short answers

## Q3. Another way in (no more audio this month)

- **Interview the crews (and the crew chief)** about what they notice at
  leak sites: wet or sunken pavement, greener grass, low pressure
  complaints, water in nearby catch basins or vaults. The 3-of-6 dug-site
  leak rate (2.9% chance if sites had been picked at random) suggests crews
  already pick sites with some skill. Writing their cues down turns that
  skill into a checklist that can be scored for all 40 sites.
- **Location data.** Map the 46 sites and test whether leaks or suspected
  leaks cluster by neighborhood, pipe age and material, soil, nearby main
  breaks or service complaints. This needs the city's asset and work-order
  records, plus GIS time.
